BOBABIE — Privacy Policy (Android)

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Android app version 1.0.0

In short

BOBABIE collects no personal data. The app asks for no account or e-mail address and contains no ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection services. It does not communicate with any server run by the developer.

Who is responsible

App publisher: Daniel Cohen — contact: danicojo@hotmail.fr

What stays on your device

BOBABIE stores only on your Android device, and never sends:

  • your saved mixes (name, chosen sounds, volumes), if you unlocked that feature;
  • settings and technical state: active sounds and volumes, chosen timer length, remaining free time, end of the pause between free sessions, and a reminder of your Premium purchase so the app starts in the right state, even without a connection.

The app turns Android backup off: this information is not included in backups. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

In-app purchase

The Premium purchase (one-time payment, no subscription) is handled by Google Play through the official Google Play Billing library. Payment and your Google account information are processed by Google under its own privacy policy; BOBABIE has no access to them and keeps nothing. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid. This is why the app declares the system network and billing permissions.

Permissions

BOBABIE uses: foreground playback (so sounds continue with the screen locked), notifications (play, pause and stop controls; you may decline), keeping the device awake during playback, and network access (only for Google Play Billing). It uses no microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID.

Children

BOBABIE collects data from no one, including children. It is intended for adults, especially parents.

Sharing with third parties

None: BOBABIE sends no data to third parties, apart from the exchanges between Google Play and your Google account for the purchase, described above.

Your rights

Because BOBABIE holds no data about you, we have nothing to access, correct or delete. To erase the local data, uninstall the app. For any question: danicojo@hotmail.fr.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated and the app declared accordingly in Google Play.
